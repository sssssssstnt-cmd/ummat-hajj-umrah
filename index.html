4 Zil Hijjah
Madinah to Aziziyah Stay

Proceed to Aziziyah accommodation for Hajj prep.

4–6 Zil Hijjah
Aziziyah Stay

Pre-Hajj guidance, spiritual orientation & rest.

7 Zil Hijjah
Proceed to Mina (After Isha)

Enter Ihram & proceed after Isha to Mina AC Tents.

9 Zil Hijjah (Day)
Day of Arafat (After Fajr)

Proceed to Plain of Arafat for primary Hajj pillar.

Proceed at Maghrib to Muzdalifah for night prayer.

10 Zil Hijjah

11 Zil Hijjah
2nd Day Ramee & Tawaf

12 Zil Hijjah
3rd Day Ramee to Aziziyah

Perform 3rd Day Ramee then transfer to Aziziyah.

13–16 Zil Hijjah
Aziziyah Stay

Perform Tawaf-e-Wida & spiritual completion.

16 Zil Hijjah
Direct Flight to India

Return home safely with 5L Zamzam & Hajj Mabrur.
